SOFTWARE PRESERVATION PROJECT

EGGStation is a non-profit, open-source educational preservation project. Its primary purpose is to research the viability of running low-latency, cycle-accurate multi-system emulation directly inside web browsers utilizing C++ WebAssembly compilation. All graphic assets, BIOS files, and ROM catalog references are intended strictly for educational preservation, backup research, and technological analysis.

100% OFFLINE LOCAL PRIVACY

Your privacy is our standard. EGGStation processes all ROM file decoders, WebAudio synthesizers, and WebGL shader pipelines locally inside your browser sandbox. No game files, SRAM saves, or diagnostic logs are ever transmitted, processed, or saved by any external web servers. Your local library is archived inside your device's browser using private IndexedDB transactions.
